# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Elite & Developing Athletes**: Talented youth, junior, and young adult athletes seeking high-performance training, tournament preparation, and professional sports pathways.
- **Guardians & Families**: Parents seeking structured athletic coaching, league participation, and verified coaching credentials for their athletes.
- **Coaches & Scouts**: Athletic directors, professional scouts, and evaluators tracking match performances, tournament statistics, and talent readiness.

## Product Purpose

ALQIMA Sports Academy (أكاديمية القمة الرياضية) serves as a high-performance sports academy and talent development pipeline in Saudi Arabia. It empowers athletes to train under certified coaches, compete in official tournaments and leagues, track athletic performance, and unlock professional scouting opportunities.

## Positioning

A premier competitive sports academy focused on elite league competition, measurable athletic progression, and scouting pathways across 7 major sports disciplines—differentiated by structured tournament preparation, professional coach-to-athlete ratios, and scout evaluation tracking.

## Operating Context

- **Environment**: Arabic-first (RTL) web application built with React 19, Vite, and Tailwind CSS v4.
- **Usage Modes**: Responsive desktop and mobile browsers used on the field, during tournament days, and by parents/athletes for registration and schedule lookups.
- **Key Workflows**: Program discovery, competitive schedule & fixture tracking, subscription enrollment, performance evaluations, and academy admissions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **7 Sports Programs**: Football, Basketball, Swimming, Karate, Kickboxing, Gymnastics, and Roller Skating with tiered skill levels up to elite/championship grade.
- **Competitive Infrastructure**: Match and training schedules, tournament tracking, and performance assessments.
- **Membership Models**: Multi-tier subscription plans (Basic, Advanced, Elite) with single and multi-sport tracks.
- **Technical & Linguistic Constraints**: Strict RTL directionality, Arabic typography (Cairo and Tajawal font pairing), high-contrast readability in athletic contexts.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: ALQIMA Sports Academy | أكاديمية القمة الرياضية
- **Visual Identity**: Bold, athletic, dynamic palette featuring Crimson Red (`#D90429`), Deep Navy (`#18213F`), and Victory Gold (`#FFC400`).
- **Voice & Tone**: Inspiring, professional, determined, championship-driven, and safety-conscious.

## Evidence on Hand

- Runnable modern frontend codebase in React 19 + TypeScript + Vite.
- Implemented data models for sports programs, coach profiles, subscription tiers, training timetables, and blog resources.
- Established brand assets, icons, and hero photography.

## Product Principles

1. **Championship Standard**: Every UI surface, feature, and interaction reflects discipline, excellence, and elite athletic standard.
2. **Clear Athletic Pathways**: Transparent progression from grassroots fundamentals to league competition and scouting visibility.
3. **Frictionless Action**: Match schedules, subscription details, and registration flows must be instant, scannable, and frictionless.
4. **Cultural & Linguistic Authenticity**: Native Arabic RTL experience designed with modern Saudi sports culture at its core.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all touchpoints.
- High color contrast for outdoor/mobile readability.
- Clear semantic HTML structure and full keyboard navigation support for Arabic RTL readers.
